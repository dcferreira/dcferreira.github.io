A desktop app that nudges you through habits (movement drills first, general habits too), shows you what to do, and logs what you actually did.

It started as a native, privacy-preserving replacement for an ad-hoc anti-sedentary setup: habit definitions, media and logs all stay on your machine,
with no network calls, no telemetry and no cloud.
Built with Tauri and Svelte, with [releases](https://github.com/dcferreira/budgebell/releases) for macOS, Linux and Windows.
